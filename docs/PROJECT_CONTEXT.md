# Krotitelé nábytku - public project context

Updated: 2026-09-05

## What the business does

Krotitelé nábytku buy, restore and sell selected Czechoslovak furniture, primarily from the 1940s-1970s. The public-facing collection focuses on notable Czech/Czechoslovak design and makers such as Halabala, Kropáček & Koželka, Šmídek and Thonet/TON where attribution is appropriate.

The business also accepts customer restoration commissions. The public website presents furniture for sale, restoration services, furniture purchasing and contact information.

## Positioning

The public positioning is based on:

- trained restoration expertise,
- knowledge of Czechoslovak furniture and attribution,
- purchase + restoration + resale under one brand,
- careful documentation of labels, manufacturers and model details,
- educational content through the website journal,
- a curated rather than general second-hand assortment.

## Content and SEO

The website has a journal (`/zapisnik/`) used for useful identification and restoration content. Existing topics include identification of Šmídek versus the so-called Céčko and the Pavouk side table.

Content should reflect real workshop expertise rather than generic SEO filler. Manufacturer labels, construction details and attribution evidence may be used when documented accurately.
