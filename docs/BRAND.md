# Brand voice

Updated: 2026-09-05

## General tone

Write in natural Czech, in complete human-sounding sentences. The tone can be warm and lightly humorous, but should not sound polished for the sake of polish.

Useful test: would this sentence sound normal if said across a workbench to a customer or colleague?

## Formatting

- In social posts and emails use a short hyphen `-` rather than an em dash.
- Website copy may use an em dash where typographically appropriate.
- A simple smiley `:)` is acceptable.
- Avoid excessive headings, slogans and fragment-heavy ad copy.

## Avoid

Do not use generic sentimental or AI-sounding phrases, especially:

- "druhá šance"
- "s láskou"
- "srdcovka"

Also avoid empty statements such as "vracíme nábytku život" unless there is a concrete reason to say something similar in context.

## Pricing and social media

Do not put prices into ordinary social posts unless explicitly requested for a specific campaign or sales post.

## Expertise

Never overstate attribution. If a maker/model is probable rather than documented, preserve that distinction. Labels, construction details and provenance should be described exactly as known.

## Customer communication

Be concise and practical. Do not over-explain a refusal or defend a purchase offer unnecessarily. For pieces outside the buying focus, a short polite decline is preferable to unsolicited advice about where else the customer should sell or dispose of the item.
