# Decisions log

Updated: 2026-09-05

## 2026-08 to 2026-09

- The curated sales collection stays focused primarily on Czechoslovak furniture from the 1940s-1970s.
- Customer restoration commissions may include suitable furniture outside that curated public collection.
- Capacity shortages should be handled by pricing and lead-time management, not by habitual overtime.
- Raw acquisition pricing must be based on the business's own ledger/market evidence where possible, not confused with retail prices after restoration.
- Standalone chairs and tables are lower-priority stock categories unless the acquisition is unusually strong; cabinets/small storage pieces deserve more attention because of faster observed turnover.
- Public attribution must distinguish documented, probable and uncertain identifications.
- Published photos must not expose EXIF/GPS metadata.
- In customer-facing social/email copy, prefer the short hyphen `-` and avoid generic sentimental brand language.
- For partially restored furniture, inspect the actual piece before firm pricing if previous work may materially change the restoration scope.
- Keep the commission business and the curated resale business operationally distinct, while letting them support the same brand.
